Selected projects

churnIQ: telecom customer churn predictionGitHub

Undergraduate research. Compared Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest on the IBM Telco dataset and calibrated the chosen model. ROC-AUC 0.845 and recall 0.714 on 1,409 held-out customers; 97 Python tests. Python, pandas, scikit-learn, FastAPI, Streamlit, Docker.

Ebyafaayo: offline Luganda oral-history keeperGitHub

Sunbird's Whisper model, converted to int8 for faster-whisper, transcribes Luganda on a laptop CPU; Gemma 4 in Ollama structures the checked transcript. Runs offline on an 8 GB laptop, with a grounding check that links every extracted name and place to its source sentence.

Eddembe: Luganda legal rights assistantLive demo

Retrieval over 373 sections of six Ugandan laws with Gemini embeddings and pgvector; Gemma 4 answers and every citation is checked against the retrieved sections. Web, Flutter Android app and USSD with SMS replies.

LUCID-Z: real-time DDoS detection (team, IBM Z Datathon 2026)GitHub

CNN that scores network flows in 10-second windows, ported to IBM LinuxONE with a NumPy inference engine. 98.68% accuracy on a 453-sample CIC-DDoS2019 test split; about 96 MB of RAM.

CodeNative: learn to code in your mother tongueLive demo

Line-by-line code explanations in Kiswahili, Luganda, Yorùbá and other languages, with Sunbird AI translation for Ugandan languages. Built for the UTAMU Hackathon, July 2026.
